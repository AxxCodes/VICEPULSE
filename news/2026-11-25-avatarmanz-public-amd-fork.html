AvatarManz opens AMD VIctory fork and reports 19–22 FPS on GTA VI’s boat mission

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Public repository and published benchmark

The maintainer reports a 41-minute session without a crash on an RX 7900 XTX. Heavy street scenes still drop to 6–10 FPS.

AvatarManz has published the source of a VIctory fork tuned for the Radeon RX 7900 XTX. Its README reports 13–20 FPS in the indoor prologue and 19–22 FPS during the first boat mission, tested at 1440p on Windows 11 with an Intel Core i9-12900K and 64 GB of RAM.

The project reports one session lasting 41 minutes without a crash. The README also shows 6–10 FPS in a busy street chase, slower individual frames, and some outdoor flicker. The 30 FPS figure applies to menus, videos and loading screens. PortWatch estimates around 16 FPS in gameplay overall. The public benchmark covers one PC.

PortWatch also lists a separate NVIDIA demo streamed by NotNozy. Its build is private and the footage does not reproduce AvatarManz’s results on another PC. The countdown still points to the open-source evidence review for repeatable longer gameplay around 30 FPS.
