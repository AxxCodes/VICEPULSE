GTA VI footage surfaces on a reported M5 Max MacBook

A 41-second clip shows GTA VI scenes on a laptop identified by the poster as an M5 Max. Character models and some textures are visible, though lighting and materials still look inconsistent.

In a clip posted by @chasmmmmmmmmmmm, the camera films a laptop screen as GTA VI cuts between outdoor and indoor scenes. Faces, hair and some patterned textures are clearly visible. Several characters have very bright, washed-out surfaces. The poster identifies the computer as an M5 Max MacBook.

The clip shows a short sequence of rendered scenes. It does not provide a reproducible build, a measured frame rate or sustained player-controlled gameplay. It gives us no reliable basis to rank Mac performance against Windows.

There is also a separate public VIctory-macOS project. Its maintainers report that their compatibility-layer test suite passes on an M4 MacBook using Wine and Metal. That is a useful result for Apple Silicon, but the developers still describe the game as unplayable, and we found no confirmed link between their repository and this M5 Max clip.
