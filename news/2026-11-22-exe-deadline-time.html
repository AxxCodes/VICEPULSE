The missing GTA VI executable is due Monday at 18:00 CET, and votes cost money

Cyberleek's countdown ends at 17:00 UTC on Nov 23 while its site charges for poll votes; the early-release token goal is reportedly down to $6 million.

Cyberleek's own countdown for the withheld executable ends on Monday, Nov 23 at 18:00 CET (17:00 UTC), and RockstarINTEL reports the same deadline. That is more precise than the "Monday" first announced, and it is the time the countdown on this page now uses.

The leaker's site pairs the timer with a poll on what happens next, and votes are cast by paying in cryptocurrency. RockstarINTEL also reports that the token goal for an early release has dropped to $6 million, after $30 million in October and $15 million earlier this week. Reports put the token at about $1.4 million.

None of this is verified. No outlet has confirmed that the group holds a working executable, and Decrypt and others describe the countdown as a way to pump the token. The group ended a similar campaign in October when its goal wasn't met.

If a file does appear, expect fakes first. Security researchers have already found fake "GTA VI installers" carrying the Vidar info-stealer and hundreds of domains pushing fake launchers. This tracker will report a release only once outlets confirm it, and will never link to it.
