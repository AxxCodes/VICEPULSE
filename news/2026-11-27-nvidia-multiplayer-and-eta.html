VIctory-NVIDIA leads at 90% as a private GTA VI demo shows multiplayer

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Public benchmark, original post, and repository status

The public fork reports 26–30 rendered FPS in opening rooms. Belobug and AvatarManz are now archived, and the stability forecast is low confidence.

VIctory-NVIDIA leads the active VICEPULSE Race at 90%. Its maintainer reports mostly 26–30 rendered FPS in the opening rooms on one RTX 5070 PC at 1440p output with DLSS Quality. That benchmark counts rendered frames separately from generated frames. The broader community figure is about 20 FPS, and the project does not give a timed run across heavier scenes.

TeK posted a roughly 38-second clip described as a private multiplayer test with characters, vehicles, proximity voice chat and 25–30 FPS. VICEPULSE verified the post and its claim. The clip provides no public build or independently repeatable frame-rate test.

The previously public Belobug and AvatarManz repositories both returned HTTP 404 when checked. Their scores remain in the archive. The 91.3% Belobug entry reflects its earlier result.

VICEPULSE’s low-confidence watch window for a public test build meeting the stability target is December 1–7. That depends on a timed test of gameplay near 30 rendered FPS across heavier scenes with a public revision and reproduction on a second PC. No developer has scheduled that milestone. The next evidence review is November 29 at 8 p.m. Martinique time.
