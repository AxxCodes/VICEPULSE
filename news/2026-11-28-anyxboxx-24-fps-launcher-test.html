AnyXboxX reports 24 FPS median in a GTA VI launcher test

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Dated maintainer report and renderer merge notes

The 14-minute run reached the 30 FPS cap in lighter scenes. Heavy scenes still fell to 10–18 FPS.

The AnyXboxX team reported a 24.4 FPS median in the game world during a 14-minute launcher session on November 27. The test used the Series X profile at 1080p output with a warm shader cache on an RTX 4070 SUPER and Core i7-13700KF. The game reached its 30 FPS cap in scenes with up to about 4,000 draw calls. Heavier scenes with roughly 7,000–11,000 calls fell to 10–18 FPS.

The benchmark notes give the in-game FPS figure without a separate rendered and generated frame count. They also give no crash count or continuous gameplay duration within the 14-minute launcher session. The same update documents mouse camera controls and an in-game performance overlay.

AnyXboxX ran GTA VI through its bundled build of the Vice-Tay renderer. A later merge updated that renderer and added frame-generation options. The 24.4 FPS measurement applies to the earlier tested build. The later merge notes leave a follow-up warm-cache gameplay comparison pending.

AnyXboxX moves from the Watchlist into the VICEPULSE Race based on the documented gameplay test. VIctory-NVIDIA remains the active leader at 90%. The next evidence review is November 29 at 8 p.m. Martinique time. December 1–7 remains a low-confidence watch window for a qualifying community build, and the release date is unknown.
