Cyberleek says the missing GTA VI executable is out, hours before its own deadline

The leaker posted the release at 15:36 CET. Nobody has confirmed the file is genuine yet, and no project has booted the game.

Cyberleek posted on Monday, Nov 23 at 15:36 CET (14:36 UTC) that the executable missing from its leaked Xbox Series build of GTA VI is now out. That is about two and a half hours before the 18:00 CET deadline the leaker had set itself. The post calls the release free and says it hands the work over to the open-source recompilation and emulation projects.

We haven't downloaded or checked the file, and we won't link to it. When we published, no outlet had confirmed it is genuine. The post also asks for crypto donations, and Cyberleek has tied earlier releases to a token, so treat the claims around it with care.

Expect fakes. Leaked files are an easy way to spread malware, and fake GTA VI downloads were circulating before this drop. Don't run executables you got from strangers, and only use the original project repositories linked in the race on our homepage.

For the race, this is the starting gun: until now no project could even try to boot GTA VI. Nothing on the tracker changes until one shows it running, and the countdown on our homepage now counts up from the release.
