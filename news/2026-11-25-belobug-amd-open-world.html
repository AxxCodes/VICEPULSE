Belobug’s AMD build renders GTA VI’s open world at 3–5 FPS

The Radeon fork shows textured streets, traffic, vegetation and water. Driving runs at about 3 FPS on an RX 9070 XT.

Belobug published a Radeon-focused fork of Marvisak’s VIctory. The maintainer reports a lit open world with textured surfaces, people, vehicles, vegetation and water on Windows 11 with an RX 9070 XT, Ryzen 7 9800X3D and 64 GB of RAM. PortWatch says it saw the build running through missions.

The maintainer reports 3–5 FPS in the world and around 3 FPS while driving. Distant trees are missing, moving vehicles can stretch or leave parts behind, and shaded areas use a flat lighting approximation. The README records a driver timeout during the test session.

Separately, hyphennn’s victory-fork reports cutting one CPU-side graphics pass from 70–220 ms to about 3 ms. That test still took 250–380 ms per frame overall, or roughly 3–4 FPS, with the GPU setting the pace.

Belobug leads VICEPULSE’s readiness ranking on the strength of its rendering. The documented crash-free gameplay checkpoint remains open.
