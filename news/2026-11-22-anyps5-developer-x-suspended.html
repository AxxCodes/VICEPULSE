AnyPS5 developer suspended on X, with no evidence Sony or Rockstar was behind it

The suspension is real, but the cause isn't public, and an account blaming the two companies also promotes a crypto token.

The X account of boykopovar, the developer of PS5-to-PC tool AnyPS5, is suspended. We saw the notice on the profile on Nov 22. It points to X's rules in general and doesn't say which one was broken or whether anyone complained.

An account describing itself as AnyPS5's community account called it a repeat suspension and suggested Sony and Rockstar fear the project. It showed no evidence that either company was involved, and we found no reporting or statement that says so. We couldn't confirm an earlier suspension either.

AnyPS5 itself is still online: new code reached its GitHub repository later that day. One project we track, americas-recomp, is a fork of it. The suspension doesn't change any scores or show progress on running GTA VI.

Be careful with what travels alongside this story. The account making the claim promotes a token using the project's name and says the developer supports it. We haven't verified that, and saw no mention of a token in the project's README or on the developer's public Telegram channel. Don't treat the suspension as a reason to buy one.
