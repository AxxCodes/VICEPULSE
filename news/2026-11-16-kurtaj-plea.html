The 2022 GTA VI hacker pleads guilty and will be sentenced on Monday

Arion Kurtaj admitted hacking Rockstar and posting about 90 development clips. His sentencing falls on the same day as Cyberleek's executable deadline.

Arion Kurtaj, the hacker behind the September 2022 leak of early GTA VI development footage, pleaded guilty at Woolwich Crown Court in London on Nov 16. He admitted hacking Rockstar Games and putting roughly 90 clips online.

The charges also cover intrusions at other companies. He is due to be sentenced at the same court on Monday, Nov 23, the same day Cyberleek says it will release the missing executable for its leaked Xbox build.

The two leaks are separate. The 2022 breach exposed unfinished footage from inside Rockstar. Cyberleek's 2026 release claims to be a finished console build of the shipped game.
