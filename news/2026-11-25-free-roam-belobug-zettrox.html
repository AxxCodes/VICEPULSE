Belobug reports faster AMD gameplay as ZETTROX’s repo goes offline

Belobug now reports 5–7 FPS in the world. A separate free-roam stream is attributed to darkworldhm, whose build author remains unknown.

Belobug’s public Radeon fork now reports 5–7 FPS in the world on its test PC. In one scene, it measured 5.4–6.7 FPS standing still and reported lower performance while driving. VICEPULSE checked the published figures. They come from one machine.

VICEPULSE checked that both the ZETTROX repository and its GitHub profile returned HTTP 404. The cause is unknown. Separately, the owner of the private omonkey build reportedly withdrew it. Both entries are now archived on VICEPULSE.

PortWatch attributes a separate Rumble free-roam stream to darkworldhm, with a reported 12–15 FPS and a peak around 18. VICEPULSE could not review the stream directly. We have no public source repository or build authorship for that demo. It remains separate from the prison clip shared by Linux4real.
