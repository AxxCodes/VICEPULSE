Cyberleek's Xbox build is out, except the file that runs it

The leaker says the missing executable will be released on Monday, Nov 23, or sooner if its meme coin reaches a $15 million market cap.

Cyberleek, the group behind this year's GTA VI footage leaks, says it has released a roughly 209 GB Xbox Series build of the game, labelled version 1.0.2.1, by torrent. The game's main executable is not included.

According to the group's statement, the executable will be released on Monday, Nov 23, with no conditions attached. It says the file will come out immediately if its CYBERLEEK token reaches a $15 million market cap first.

No outlet or security researcher has independently verified that the files are a genuine GTA VI build, and Rockstar and Take-Two have not commented. Projects on this tracker say they are working with a package whose contents match what they expect, including 26,503 shaders, but none can boot it without the executable.

Cyberleek also predicted that PC players would be emulating the build within a week. As Gamereactor points out, no working Xbox Series X|S emulator exists yet. That gap is exactly what the projects on this page are trying to close.
