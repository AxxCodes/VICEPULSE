CBL VIctory and VIctory-RDNA document longer GTA VI PC runs

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Dated maintainer results, run logs and frame-generation settings

CBL reports a clean exit after 72 minutes of uptime on an RTX 5080. VIctory-RDNA logs a 20.8 rendered FPS median on an RX 7900 XT.

CBL VIctory, a fork using Vice-Tay’s renderer, recorded 72 minutes and 53 seconds of process uptime on an RTX 5080 at 1440p output. The November 28 results describe a manual controller playthrough from the prison through early missions and into free roam, followed by an idle check, a save and a clean exit. Across about 59 minutes of gameplay and cutscenes before idle, the reported median was 14.5 rendered FPS. Frame generation was off. The night drive dropped to a 10.3 FPS median, and dark patches on water remained visible.

VIctory-RDNA reports a 23-minute, 50-second capture on an RX 7900 XT using the Series X profile at 4K output. It recorded a median of 20.8 rendered FPS across 260 qualified samples and 20.3 FPS in 144 heavier-scene samples. The run ended cleanly, and the project lists these benchmark figures with frame generation off.

Both projects enter the VICEPULSE Race on the strength of their published results. These runs show that the builds can stay open through longer tests on the machines used. A public build still needs to sustain around 30 rendered FPS through longer gameplay across heavier scenes and repeat the result on a second PC. The next evidence review is November 29 at 8 p.m. Martinique time. December 1–7 remains a low-confidence watch window for a qualifying community build, and the release date is unknown.
