Victory-4080Super reports around 40 rendered FPS in a two-minute GTA VI sample

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Dated project results, sampling windows and build settings

The 4K readings came from one area during a 36-minute run. A separate Proton fork reports a 33 FPS mean in its own boat-mission test.

Victory-4080Super’s November 27 Run 10 lasted 36 minutes and ended without a reported crash. On an RTX 4080 SUPER with frame generation off and 3840 × 2160 output, its in-game overlay showed 40.9, 38.4 and 40.1 rendered FPS, with 1% lows of 29–31 FPS. Those three readings span about two minutes in one area. The report gives no session-wide FPS average.

An earlier run on the same PC lasted about 87 minutes and showed 38.1 rendered FPS in one night-city reading. Some overlay samples had single-digit 1% lows when the world failed to stream around the camera. The newer run still showed flat character textures and intermittent flashing.

Separately, the Linux fork of VIctory-NVIDIA reports a rise from 30.8 to 33.0 FPS mean across alternating windows in one run of the boat mission on an RTX 5070 Ti under Proton. The comparison isolates a setting that held GPU clocks up between submissions. The notes give no separate generated-frame count or continuous near-30 FPS duration for that run.

Victory-4080Super joins the Watchlist and the Proton fork enters the Race on its reported gameplay test. VIctory-NVIDIA still leads at 90%. A timed near-30 rendered FPS result across heavier scenes with repeatable results on another PC is still needed. The next evidence review is November 29 at 8 p.m. Martinique time. The December 1–7 watch window remains low confidence, and the release date is unknown.
