Belobug takes the GTA VI PC lead with a reported 22 FPS average

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Public repository and separate city benchmark

Belobug moves to 91.3% overall. A community test reports no crashes on an RX 9070 XT, with the session duration still unknown.

Belobug now leads the VICEPULSE Race at 91.3%, ahead of CGBJ at 89.6% overall and AvatarManz at 81.8% in the public race. A community report puts Belobug at about 22 FPS on average on an RX 9070 XT and describes a run with no crashes, using around 30 GB of system memory and 12 GB of video memory. The report does not provide the session length.

The current public README documents a separate result: about 22 FPS in a city-street test on an RX 7900 XTX, using the Series S profile with ray tracing off. That benchmark measures one scene on one PC. The 80% story estimate is part of the tracker assessment.

The countdown still points to the scheduled evidence review. A timed run near 30 FPS across gameplay on a public build would give a clearer indication of stability.
