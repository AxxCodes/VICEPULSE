Exclusive: CGBJ gets GTA VI in game, walking and talking in an unlit world

A private build based on VIctory and XCL lets a player walk around and talk to NPCs, very slowly. A video shows the opening scenes in flat colours, without lighting.

A group called CGBJ has got GTA VI into its game world on PC, and a player can move in it. With a controller they walk around, talk to NPCs and open the in-game menu. A third-party tracker reported that the build gets in game. No public project has shown a player moving yet.

It is far from playable, and it runs very slowly. In the run where a player walked around, the screen was black. A 77-second video posted on X on Tuesday shows more: GTA VI's opening scenes in flat colours with no lighting, from a car's interior to a street of waving NPCs, a guard and a prison yard. The image above, a debug view that colours each surface by the direction it faces, shows that yard from a doorway.

CGBJ's code is private. It builds on VIctory, the open-source layer whose author first showed GTA VI's menus on a livestream on Monday, and also takes some of XCL's work. Both are public on GitHub; CGBJ's changes aren't, so nobody outside the group can test them.

We've added CGBJ to the tracker at 72%, at our In-game stage, which puts it first. That rests on our own check: the video doesn't name the project, and nobody outside the group can reproduce the result yet.
