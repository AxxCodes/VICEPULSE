DexNom publishes a ray-tracing benchmark for GTA VI’s City scene

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Publication of the Radeon benchmark

The public AMD fork reports 1.03 FPS with experimental ray tracing enabled on an RX 7900 XTX.

VICEPULSE checked the published City benchmark. DexNom compared 50 frames from the same autosave before and after a memory-tracking fix. The team reports a rise from 0.29 to 1.03 FPS. Its diagnostic counter also recorded rays intersecting scene geometry. Lighting on individual objects remains under investigation.

A separate indoor test in the same report went from 3.52 to 4.05 FPS. These tests cover different scenes and settings. Neither measures long-session stability.
