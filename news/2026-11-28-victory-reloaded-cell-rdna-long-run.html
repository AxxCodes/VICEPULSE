VIctory-reloaded reports 29.6 FPS in the cell as a longer Radeon run slows

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Dated maintainer measurements and benchmark settings

The RTX 4090 test covered one prologue scene. VIctory-RDNA’s 37-minute 4K capture on an RX 7900 XT logged a 15.35 rendered FPS median.

VIctory-reloaded’s November 28 notes report a 29.6 FPS median in a 12-minute session on an RTX 4090. The Series S test stayed in the prologue cell. Across three separate warm launches, the new build recorded 28.8 FPS over the first settled minute in the cell, compared with 17.4 FPS on the Vice-Tay revision the fork started from. The figures come from the game’s frame-rate logs. The measurement notes give no separate generated or presented FPS figure.

VIctory-RDNA’s extended November 28 capture logged 37 minutes and 28 seconds from startup to a clean window close. At 4K output on an RX 7900 XT with the RDNA3 Fast preset, the maintainer reports a median of 15.35 rendered FPS across 410 gameplay samples and 14.7 FPS in heavier samples. Frame generation was off. The gameplay-sample median fell from 19.8 FPS in the first five minutes to 13.5 in the final five. Workload also changed, and the log does not establish the cause of the slowdown.

That Radeon run used a different preset from the earlier 23-minute capture at 20.8 rendered FPS, so the two medians cannot quantify a regression. The new tests sharpen the evidence for one fast scene and one longer session, respectively. VICEPULSE’s stability target still requires extended gameplay around 30 rendered FPS and a repeat on another PC. The next evidence review remains November 29 at 8 p.m. Martinique time. December 1–7 is a low-confidence watch window, with the release date unknown.
