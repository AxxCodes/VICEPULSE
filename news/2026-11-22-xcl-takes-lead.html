XCL takes the lead after running its first retail Xbox games on PC

The Wine-style layer says Minecraft Preview is fully playable on Windows. GTA VI still can't start, because its executable is missing.

XCL runs Xbox Series code natively and swaps in its own versions of the console's system libraries. On Nov 22 its README began reporting that Minecraft Preview is fully playable on a PC, with worlds, sound and a controller. Fortnite and XDefiant reach their title screens and menus, going as far as they can without their online services.

Its commit history over the day matches that account: Fortnite's splash screen in the morning, Minecraft's main menu by early afternoon and a playable build by the evening. The results are self-reported, and nobody else has reproduced them yet.

For GTA VI, XCL says all 26,698 GPU programs in the game's shader pack translate to HLSL and that sampled ones compile for PC. Running another retail game end to end is the strongest sign yet that a layer like this could carry GTA VI. XCL's score rises to about 22%, ahead of xbox-emulator.

Elsewhere, americas-recomp now translates almost all of the game's shaders, and Leonida's loader runs a stand-in program end to end. Every project is still waiting on the same file.
