omonkey reportedly reaches GTA VI’s prison scene at about 5 FPS

PortWatch VI reports character movement and partial shader rendering in a private Windows build. It also reports progress on Five Star Recomp.

PortWatch VI reports that omonkey’s build shows movement around the opening prison area at roughly 5 FPS, with some shaders beginning to render. It points to development streams on Swaggely’s Rumble channel. The build is private, and there is no public test that establishes longer sessions.

PortWatch VI placed the run at 76%. On VICEPULSE’s published rubric, reaching in-game at a reported 5 FPS with unknown stability scores 72.5%, just ahead of CGBJ at 72%. These are tracker points and use different scoring judgments.

Five Star Recomp’s public project also reports reaching the story intro’s title card before a GPU frame stalls. VICEPULSE records its completed menu milestone at 55%. The Windows countdown still targets November 30 as a low-confidence checkpoint for a publicly documented 30-minute crash-free session.
