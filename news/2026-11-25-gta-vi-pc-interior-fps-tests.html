GTA VI PC clips show faster interiors, with heavier scenes still slower

Confirmed by VICEPULSE · Original X footage and sampled counter readings

A prison clip shared by Linux4real shows sampled readings around 10–12 FPS. The build author is unknown. The separate victory-perf fork publishes its own numbers.

Linux4real (@Linux5real) shared a 29-second clip on X showing movement in Lucia’s prison area. The post claims 12–15 FPS and lists a GeForce RTX 5070 Ti and Ryzen 7 7800X3D in a reply. VICEPULSE saw readings around 10–12 FPS in several sampled frames. The footage does not establish who developed the build.

Separately, the public victory-perf fork reports 14 FPS in a prison corridor, 11 FPS inside the heist and 9 FPS in the cell. Heavier scenes run at 4.5–6.5 FPS. The maintainer used one RTX 4070 SUPER system at 50% render scale and approximated some missing lighting effects.

The clip stays in the news as footage of an unidentified build. Only the separate victory-perf project gets a named ranking from its benchmark. The open-source stability review still requires longer, repeatable sessions.
