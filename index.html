    We are tracking a publicly available build that can hold around 30 FPS through extended gameplay on more than one PC. The clock counts down to our next evidence review.

    What would establish stability?

    VIctory-NVIDIA reports 26–30 rendered FPS in its opening-room test. A separate 25-minute open-world test averaged about 17 FPS as reported by the fxceless maintainer. Vice-Tay and VI-BR-PORT report short boat runs near 30 FPS. The longer CBL VIctory and VIctory-RDNA tests add evidence on session length. A new 37-minute Radeon run logged a 15.35 rendered FPS median. Victory-4080Super’s readings around 40 rendered FPS cover about two minutes in one area. foxhound’s scripted driving test recorded about 30 rendered FPS at the median on one PC. Earlier heavier gameplay on that project ran at 16–21 FPS. The next evidence review is November 29 at 8 p.m. Martinique time. December 1–7 is a low-confidence watch window for a qualifying public build. The release date remains unknown.

    Recent tests show double-digit FPS indoors and a fuller open world on AMD. Those are results from different builds. Heavier scenes still run slower, and longer sessions need repeatable tests.

    A public source revision, timed sessions with frame rates and crash results, and reproduction on a second machine would support a stability assessment. Private builds and short clips cannot establish a release date.
