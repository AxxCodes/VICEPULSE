Estimated checkpoint November 30, 2026 Early window Nov 28–Dec 7

The first target is a publicly documented 30-minute gameplay session without a crash on one PC. This is an unofficial forecast.

How the estimate works

PortWatch VI’s leader climbed from 15.8% on Nov 21 to 55% on Nov 23 and 72% on Nov 24. CGBJ reportedly reached the world at around 4 FPS, with lighting missing. VIctory reports 30 FPS in menus and world loading, with rendering still failing in gameplay.

A fix for a major crash could unlock a long session quickly. November 30 is a six-day editorial checkpoint based on the pace of those early milestones. The Nov 28–Dec 7 window assumes teams can resolve their current blockers, and may slip if crashes recur elsewhere in the game.

We’ll move the forecast when there’s a dated gameplay recording or repeatable test with the build, hardware, session length and crash behavior documented.

Nov 24 update: omonkey’s reported 5 FPS prison test shows additional rendering progress. The MacBook clip also looks promising. Neither establishes the 30-minute crash-free Windows session this clock is tracking, so November 30 remains the checkpoint.

          Methodology

          How we score

          Groundwork makes up the first 25 points. After that, the ladder measures what each project achieves in GTA VI. The headline score belongs to the leading active community effort.

            Groundwork, weighted

              Past "In-game", the last 30 points split between frame rate (average FPS ÷ 30) and stability (from crashes often up to stable). Partial milestones, such as a menu that renders but is broken, count half.

              Maintain a project and think it's mis-scored? Open an issue with a link to your evidence.
